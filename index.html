Privacy Policy — Taut

Last updated: 5 October 2026

Taut does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising, and no network connection of any kind.

Microphone

Taut needs the microphone for one reason: to hear the note you are playing so it can measure its pitch. Audio is analysed in real time, in memory, on your device, and discarded immediately. Nothing is recorded, nothing is written to disk, and nothing is sent anywhere.

Data stored on your device

Your settings (reference pitch, tolerance, note spelling, haptics) and any custom tunings you create are saved on your device so they are there next time. They never leave it. Deleting the app deletes them.

Third parties

Taut contains no third-party SDKs, trackers, or analytics. No data is shared with anyone, because none is collected.

Children

Taut collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new date.
